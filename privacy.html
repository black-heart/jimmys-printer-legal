Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Overview

Jimmy's Printer ("we", "our", or "the app") is built by BillionStars. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Child's name and age — stored locally on your device only, used to personalize worksheets
  • Custom text — any words or names you type for practice sheets, stored locally only

Automatically Collected

  • Purchase receipts — RevenueCat processes subscription transactions; we receive only the subscription status (active/expired), not payment details
  • Print usage count — a daily counter stored locally to manage the free tier limit (1 print/day)

How We Use Information

  • Generate personalized worksheets and practice sheets
  • Verify subscription status for Pro features
  • Enforce the free-tier daily limit

Data Storage

All personal data stays on your device. We do not operate a server. Your child's name, age, custom text, and print history are stored locally via:

  • AsyncStorage (settings, history)
  • SQLite (subscription cache)

Third-Party Services

  • RevenueCat — handles subscription billing. See RevenueCat Privacy Policy
  • Apple AirPrint — printing is handled by iOS; we do not intercept print content

Children's Privacy

Jimmy's Printer is designed for parents to use with their children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. All data entered is provided by the parent/guardian and stored locally.

Data Deletion

You can delete all app data at any time by uninstalling the app. All local data is removed upon uninstallation.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy occasionally. Changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date.

Contact
If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at support@billionstars.com.
